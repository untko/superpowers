# Creative

Use this family entrypoint for creative visual work.

When the request needs architecture diagrams, HTML/SVG diagrams, Excalidraw,
Mermaid, or a tldraw canvas (sketching one, or reading and organizing one),
read and follow [Visual Design](visual-design/SKILL.md).

Landing pages, app UI, components, and other frontend design belong to the
third-party `impeccable` skill, not this family.
